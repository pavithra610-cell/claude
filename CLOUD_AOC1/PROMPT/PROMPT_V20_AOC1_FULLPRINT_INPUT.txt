FORM AOC-1 — FULL TRANSCRIPTION AND ₹-CRORE RESTATEMENT OF ONE INDIAN FILER'S STATEMENT OF SUBSIDIARIES, ASSOCIATES AND JOINT VENTURES

You are given a PDF carve from an Indian company's annual report or MCA filing that contains the
company's Form AOC-1 (Companies Act 2013, s.129(3), Companies (Accounts) Rules 2014 r.5): the
statement of salient features of the financial statements of its subsidiaries (Part A) and of its
associate companies and joint ventures (Part B). Your task has two parts, kept strictly separate:

  PART 1 — RECORD: transcribe every row of Part A and Part B exactly as printed, every printed
           column, every note, with the denomination evidence that governs each row. No arithmetic,
           no conversion, no re-scaling in Part 1. Brackets stay brackets, dashes become null,
           digits keep their printed grouping.
  PART 2 — RESTATE: for every row, restate the key money fields in ₹ crore using ONLY what Part 1
           recorded, writing the exact arithmetic beside each number so a human can check it.

Everything below is the accumulated knowledge of how Indian filers print this form, how the page
misleads a careful reader, and how the two parts must be kept apart. Read it all before the PDF.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. WHAT THE FORM IS — STATUTORY LINES, TITLES, LAYOUTS, SCOPE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 STATUTORY PART A LINES (16 particulars; filers reword them — map every printed header to one):
  A01 Sl. No.
  A02 Name of the subsidiary
  A03 Date since when subsidiary was acquired / date of incorporation
  A04 Reporting period for the subsidiary, if different from the holding company's
  A05 Reporting currency and exchange rate as on the last date of the relevant financial year
      (foreign subsidiaries) — some filers print TWO rates: closing (balance sheet) and average (P&L)
  A06 Share capital
  A07 Reserves & surplus ("Reserves and Surplus", "Other equity")
  A08 Total assets
  A09 Total liabilities (many filers print this GROSS = equal to total assets, i.e. including equity;
      others print external liabilities only — both conventions are normal)
  A10 Investments ("Investments other than in subsidiaries")
  A11 Turnover ("Total income", "Revenue from operations", "Revenue", "Gross turnover")
  A12 Profit before taxation ("Profit/(Loss) before tax")
  A13 Provision for taxation ("Tax expense", "Current + deferred tax")
  A14 Profit after taxation
  A15 Proposed dividend ("Proposed dividend / earnings transfer to owners")
  A16 Extent of shareholding (%) ("% of shareholding", "% holding")
  Common extra columns (keep, key X_): Country; CIN/registration no.; Relationship / s.2(87) basis;
  Instruments entirely equity in nature; Share application money; OCI; Total comprehensive income;
  Voting power %; Financial year end; Notes.
1.2 STATUTORY PART B LINES (associates and joint ventures):
  B01 Name of associate / joint venture
  B02 Latest audited balance sheet date
  B03 Date on which the associate / JV was associated or acquired
  B04 Shares held by the company on the year end: B04a Number · B04b Amount of investment · B04c Extent of holding %
  B05 Description of how there is significant influence
  B06 Reason why the associate / JV is not consolidated
  B07 Networth attributable to shareholding as per latest audited balance sheet
  B08 Profit / loss for the year: B08a Considered in consolidation · B08b Not considered in consolidation
  Part B notes: names yet to commence operations; names liquidated or sold during the year.
1.3 THE TITLE IS NOT THE TEST. Filers call it "Form AOC-1", "FORM NO. AOC-1", "AOC-I", "AOC 1",
  "Annexure-A/B/VI", "Statement pursuant to Section 129(3)", "Salient Features of Financial
  Statements of Subsidiaries", or give it no heading (Borosil Renewables prints "A. Salient Features
  of Financial Statements of Subsidiaries" with no "AOC" string anywhere). The statement is THE TABLE
  OF NAMED SUBSIDIARIES WITH FINANCIAL FIGURES. Content decides.
1.4 LOOK-ALIKES THAT ARE NOT THE FORM (record their presence in ignored_non_aoc_content, never
  transcribe them as Part A/B): the Schedule III "share of each entity in consolidated net assets /
  profit / OCI / TCI" table (% columns, amounts are group-consolidation shares — Reliance prints it
  beside the AOC-1; HCL prints it on the facing page); Form AOC-2 (related-party contracts);
  related-party (Ind AS 24) note; Ind AS 112 summarised financials; instrument-wise investment
  schedules (shares / MF / CCPS / CCD / NCD lists with quantities and face values); CSR annexure;
  Section 186 loans-guarantees annexure; MGT-9 / MGT-7 extracts; NCI note; auditor "Other Matter"
  paragraph. Only the single "Investments" column of Part A and B04b of Part B are AOC investment fields.
1.5 LAYOUTS: HORIZONTAL (one row per subsidiary, particulars as columns); TRANSPOSED / VERTICAL
  (one COLUMN per subsidiary, particulars down the left, a constant Sl. No. 1–16 that indexes the
  PARTICULARS, not the subsidiaries — Persistent, GMM Pfaudler, Greenlam, PPL Pharma, Elgi);
  ONE PAGE PER SUBSIDIARY (small filers' template forms). Sequences may RESTART (Uflex prints two
  groups each from Sr.1) or CONTINUE from a higher number; Sr. numbers may be absent altogether.
  Part B is sometimes printed as named columns rather than numbered rows.
1.6 HEADER SCOPE DECIDES WHETHER PART B EXISTS. Read the statement's own heading verbatim:
  (a) "...of subsidiaries / associate companies / joint ventures" → Part B is expected; if absent,
      record part_b.status = MISSING_EXPECTED.
  (b) "...of subsidiaries" only (Symega, CEAT, Persistent, LTTS, Quess, HCL) → the form is scoped to
      subsidiaries by design → part_b.status = NOT_IN_SCOPE_OF_HEADER. Nothing is missing.
  (c) Part B printed with "Nil", "Not applicable", or the title struck through → NIL_AS_PRINTED, quote it.
  (d) Part B printed with rows → transcribe.
1.7 CONTROL, NOT EQUITY %, DECIDES A vs B: a 49%-equity / 51%-voting entity is a subsidiary (Prime
  Pharma SPA Algeria); 45.95% equity / 50% voting is an associate. Record voting % where printed;
  never re-classify a row — record it in the Part the filer put it in and note any voting column.
1.8 ANNEXURES ARE NOT BOUND IN LETTER ORDER (Madurai Power: B, C, then A which is the AOC-1). Two-up
  spread pages carry two printed pages on one PDF page, sometimes rotated 90°, sometimes with the
  AOC-1 half beside an unrelated annexure (CSR, AOC-2, another entity's balance sheet). Transcribe
  only the AOC-1 half; list the other half in ignored_non_aoc_content. A lead page before the
  heading (notes, board report, auditor sign-off) is context only.
1.9 PER-ROW VINTAGE. A table is not automatically current-year: MMTC's FY2024-25 table carries a
  subsidiary at FY2022-23 ("FS not received"), JVs at FY2024-25, FY2020-21 and FY2016-17. Record
  A04 / B02 / any "period" cell per row exactly; stub periods (18-Oct-2024 to 31-Mar-2025) are
  recorded as printed, never annualised.

2. DENOMINATION — THE THREE STRATA, THE THREE FORMATS, AND WHERE THE UNIT HIDES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 THREE STRATA, recorded separately, never merged:
  S1 UNIT MENTIONS THAT BIND VALUES: any scale word (crore/lakh/million/thousand), currency word or
     symbol, or figure embedded in headers, second header lines ("Foreign currencies in Million"),
     except-clauses ("All figures except exchange rates are in Crores" — Marico), footers, column
     titles, name cells, or a dedicated unit row INSIDE the table body (McLeod Russel: each of four
     subsidiary columns stamps "` in Lakhs" mid-table while the page header lost the ₹ glyph;
     Alps Industries row 2, CL Educate row 3, Roto row 2, Medicamen / Khadim per-column titles).
  S2 THE REPORTING-CURRENCY COLUMN — a SELECTOR: tells which S1 rule governs each row and, in
     twin-row layouts, which twin is native and which is INR. It is not itself a denomination.
  S3 THE EXCHANGE-RATE COLUMN / BLOCK — REFERENCE: the historical conversion price. Recorded
     verbatim, never applied in Part 1; applied in Part 2 only where §2.3 says values are native.
2.2 THREE FORMATS (state one per table, with the evidence lines verbatim):
  FORMAT_1 — one INR unit governs every value; the currency and rate columns are notional. About
     three quarters of all statements. Canon: Tata Steel "(₹ crore)" — "105 Tata Steel UK Limited
     GBP 110.47 35,450.10 …" means share capital ₹35,450.10 crore, not £35,450. Also: Indian Oil
     (₹ crore, closing + average rates listed = already translated); Dion (₹ lakh, two rates).
  FORMAT_2 — dual: an INR unit for Indian rows and a foreign-currency unit (usually Million) for
     foreign rows, selected by S2; OR each foreign subsidiary printed twice (native row + INR row);
     OR native and INR COLUMNS side by side. Canon: Reliance Industries — header "(₹ in crore)" plus
     "Foreign Currencies in Million"; foreign subs starred, "10 Addverb Technologies USA Inc. * USD
     7.00 (4.78) 9.83" = USD 7.00 million share capital; rate block below the table "1 USD=85.475,
     1 GBP=110.7025 … 100 JPY=56.7575". Hindalco: every foreign sub as an INR-crore row + FC-million
     row. UltraTech: FOUR rows per subsidiary (CY native, CY ₹, PY native, PY ₹), "(Amount in
     crores)" binding only the ₹ rows. Jubilant Ingrevia: native-ABSOLUTE row with no label followed
     by an UNNAMED ₹-million row — the name flows down, the unnamed row is the INR twin. Symega:
     USD column with closing 85.45 and average 83.68 beside an INR column. Marico: single scale
     (crore) but currency varies by row (BDT-crore twin beside INR-crore twin).
  FORMAT_3 — per-cell / per-column units, or no unit printed anywhere, or conflicting signals.
     RN Chidakashi (transposed): row 3 reads column by column "US Dollars (In Millions)" / "UK
     Pounds" / "Rupees (In Lakhs)" / "US Dollars" — four units in one table. U K Paints (Berger,
     scanned): unit inside the NAME cell — "(Fig in Thousands)", "(Fig in Lakhs)", "(Fig in USD)",
     "(Fig in Crores)", "(Fig in GBP)" — five units. Zoho: "amounts in Lakhs" header + per-row
     currency and rate columns, native-lakh × rate, with individual share-capital cells absolute and
     one row stamped "INR in Thousands". eMudhra: two domestic columns ₹-million, six foreign
     columns native-absolute with per-column rates, one IDR column in millions. Hero Motors: ₹-abs
     domestic, GBP native ×1e5, THB native — three bases. QBurst: CY column ₹-million, PY column
     ₹-lakh in the same table.
2.3 THE #1 TRAP — THE STATUTORY CAPTION IS NOT THE UNIT. "(Information in respect of each
  subsidiary to be presented with amounts in Rs.)" / "…in ₹" / "…in INR" / "Amounts in Rs." is the
  form's own instruction, printed on every AOC-1. A scale word ANYWHERE in header, parenthetical,
  footer or table body overrides it: NMDC (real = crore), Transpek (lakhs), Windlas (millions),
  Supreme Nonwoven (lakhs), Zenex (millions), Rayzon (million), Narmada Bio-Chem (lakhs), Promac
  (lakhs), Meril (lakhs), Sanathan ("(in Lakhs)" beats a prose "rupees one thousand crores"), Surya
  Roshni (header "in lakhs" governs the rows; footer "in crore" covers only the FC conversion note),
  HMA Agro ("(in millions)" vs "(* in '000)" — the one sitting in the sub-table header governs).
  ABSOLUTE rupees only when NO scale word appears anywhere — the signal is large full-digit numbers
  in Indian grouping (1,42,63,588). Small-filer template forms with only the boilerplate caption are
  decided per filer by magnitude: Freight Consolidators — a GmbH share capital "2,75,000" cannot be
  ₹2.75 lakh (German minimum €25,000; €275k classic) → native × printed rate; Covalense — same
  template, ₹-absolute (a native reading gave ~₹1,300 crore for a small group; ₹-abs fits ~₹84 crore).
  When the share-capital norm and the turnover-size test disagree, turnover size wins.
2.4 WHERE THE UNIT PRINTS (verbatim strings seen): (₹ in crore) · (₹ in crores) · (` in crore) ·
  ` (Crore) · (Rs. in Lakhs) · (Rs in Lakhs) · Rs. In lakhs · (*Lakhs) · * in Lacs · (₹in lakhs) ·
  (Amount in Lakhs) · (INR Mn) · (INR in crore) · (in INR million) · (Rs. in million) · (C in
  million) · (in ` millions) · (in ` '000) · In Crores) · Amount in ` Crore · "Unless otherwise
  specified, all monetary values are in Lakhs of INR" · "All amounts are in ₹ Million, unless
  otherwise stated" · "…amount in ₹ in Lakhs" (carries a scale) · footer "Foreign Currencies in
  Million" · "Indian rupee equivalents of the figures given in foreign currencies…" · "*Converted
  using closing exchange rate; # Converted using average exchange rate" · "The Foreign Currency
  Figures have been converted into Indian Rupees using [rate]".
2.5 THE RUPEE GLYPH IS AN OPEN LIST. Font substitution renders ₹ as ` (backtick), C, H, I, J, K, *,
  2, ~ — differently within one document by font run (Tata Chemicals, Bharti Airtel, Tata Comm,
  Apollo Tyres, Star). "C in million" = ₹ in million; "all amounts in 2 crores" = ₹ crores; a double
  space in "amounts in  Lakhs" is a dropped glyph, not an absent unit. Any implausible single
  character where a currency symbol belongs is the rupee sign. Variants: Lakh/Lakhs/Lacs/Lac;
  Crore/Crores/Cr.; Million/Millions/Mn.
2.6 TWO-RATE SIGNATURE. A table printing BOTH "Exchange rate (P&L average)" AND "Exchange rate
  (BS closing)" has ALREADY translated to INR at the header scale (BS at closing, P&L at average per
  Ind AS 21) — the rates are descriptive. A NATIVE table prints ONE closing rate "as on the last
  date of the financial year" for the reader to apply. Record both rates whenever printed and state
  which column the figures come from.
2.7 A CAPTION MAY BELONG TO THE NEIGHBOURING TABLE (Kalyan Jewellers: a "₹ in million" line on the
  page belonged to another table; the AOC-1 was ₹-absolute). Bind a caption only to the table it
  heads. A per-column unit note may be bogus (Grasim: a ×0.1 column note against ₹-crore columns).
2.8 BRACKETS AND ANNOTATIONS. Round brackets = negative. Square brackets carry two meanings on the
  same page (Dorf-Ketal): on foreign rows [₹ x] is the rupee equivalent of the native figure beside
  it; on Indian rows [x] is negative — decided by whether a ₹ symbol sits inside. "[Refer Note]" or
  "*" in a cell displaces the value (Lupin) — record the annotation, do not slide neighbours.
  "#REF!" printed in a cell (Neeyamo) is null. A rate cell printing "0.00" (Dabur PARS Iran, CCL
  Vietnam) is a placeholder, not a rate. Mixed digit grouping in one number ("1,917,13,965.17") is
  read by magnitude against its row; note it.
2.9 DUPLICATE NAMES ARE REAL (Black Box: "AGC Networks LLC" ×3 and a duplicated Sr.70; Adani Green:
  two pairs with different figures; Alkem; Thomas Cook). Record every printed row; never merge.
2.10 SIZE PRIOR — for stating a doubt, never for deciding a value: entity-formation capital norms
  (GmbH ≥ €25,000; US par $1,000–5,000; DMCC AED 50,000; Pte SGD 1–100,000); a subsidiary's turnover
  above 1.5× the parent's consolidated revenue is implausible EXCEPT for agency/aggregator groups
  that bill gross while the parent books net commission (EaseMyTrip: YoloBus ₹674 crore gross GMV);
  a holding company's own turnover is tiny while the group under it is large (scope, not unit).
  Where headers are silent, list the candidate readings {INR@scale, native@scale, native-absolute}
  and say which the magnitudes support — as a note, with FORMAT_3 and null in Part 2.

3. PART 1 — TRANSCRIPTION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 PAGE MAP FIRST. For each carve page: what it carries (heading, Part A rows range, Part B,
  notes/signatures, non-AOC content), PDF page and printed folio, and the first line as proof.
  Record the filer name and CIN as printed on the page (folder names are claims; the page is proof).
3.2 DENOMINATION LOCK SECOND (before any row): S1 lines verbatim (header, mid-table, footer),
  S2 role, S3 block verbatim, format class, INR unit, foreign unit, one line of evidence.
3.3 ROSTER THIRD: every subsidiary name in printed order with its printed Sr. (or position if no
  Sr.), and the printed count. The roster anchors everything that follows.
3.4 ROWS — THREE LAYERS PER SUBSIDIARY OBJECT, every row, in printed order:
  (i)  the printed headers as keys, values exactly as printed (Indian grouping kept, brackets kept,
       "-" / "–" / blank → null, "Nil" → "Nil"); two-line headers joined with a space; CY/PY twins
       suffixed with the printed period; in TRANSPOSED layout the particulars column supplies the keys
       and each subsidiary column becomes one object;
  (ii) "canonical": all sixteen A-codes present on every row (null where the filer did not print
       that line), same printed strings re-keyed; extra columns under X_ keys;
  (iii) "denomination_for_this_row": the unit and currency governing THIS row's figures, e.g.
       "INR CRORE" / "USD MILLION" / "INR LAKH" / "EUR ABSOLUTE" / "UNRESOLVED", from §2 plus any
       per-row stamp; "row_kind": SINGLE / NATIVE / INR_EQUIVALENT / CY / PY; "reporting_currency_raw",
       "exchange_rate_raw" (closing and average if both), "footnote_marks", "row_period_as_printed",
       "row_notes" (annotations, [Refer Note], #REF!, 0.00 rate, duplicate-name flag, voting %).
  Column position comes from the printed grid, never from reading order: a truly blank cell keeps
  its column (Thomas Cook: blank cells shift every later value left in text order). A value that
  would make a row "balance" but is not printed is not written (Wipro row 142).
3.5 EVERY ROW, EVERY TIME. 308 subsidiaries = 308 objects (Motherson); 272 (Reliance); 149 (M&M);
  148 (Wipro); 141 (Tech Mahindra). Continue across pages, spreads and rotations. If output must
  end early, end after a complete object and write "truncated_after": the last name — the roster
  already carries the full list. After the last row: "part_a_rows_emitted" and
  "part_a_roster_vs_rows_match".
3.6 PART B under the same three layers with B-codes; status per §1.6; Part B printed as columns is
  transcribed column by column.
3.7 NOTES VERBATIM: every footnote symbol and its meaning; yet-to-commence list; liquidated / sold /
  merged / ceased list (with the filer's own markers, e.g. "* amalgamated with…", "^ liquidated");
  "no subsidiary was liquidated…" sentences; signature block date, place, signatory count.
3.8 SCOPE LINE. The task message may restrict the range: "SCOPE: subsidiary rows at roster
  positions 1–150 (= printed Sr 1–150 where printed) …" or "…from roster position 151 to the end,
  then Part B, notes, Part 2 for those rows, completion". Page map, denomination lock and the FULL
  roster are output in every request regardless; row objects and Part 2 objects only for the
  positions named. Without a SCOPE line, transcribe everything.

4. PART 2 — ₹-CRORE RESTATEMENT (the only place arithmetic is allowed)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 ONE TRANSFORMATION PER ROW. Each printed value reaches ₹ crore by exactly one transformation,
  determined by the row's denomination_for_this_row. Serving errors are never random — they are a
  DOUBLE conversion (a rate or scale applied to a value the filer already converted) or a PHANTOM
  conversion (a transformation the page does not print). Never stack: rate × scale is legal only
  when the page prints native values AND a scale for those native values.
4.2 UNIT FACTORS TO CRORE: crore ×1 · lakh ÷100 · million ÷10 · thousand ÷1,00,000 · absolute
  ÷1,00,00,000.
4.3 BY FORMAT:
  FORMAT_1 → value × unit factor. No exchange rate (the rate is descriptive). A two-rate table is
  FORMAT_1 by definition (§2.6).
  FORMAT_2 twin rows / twin columns → the INR member IS the restated value ("method": PRINTED_INR);
  the native member is evidence and is never converted.
  FORMAT_2 single native row (Reliance foreign rows) → value × foreign unit factor × printed rate
  (₹ per unit; a "100 JPY = 56.7575" rate is ÷100 first). Use the closing rate for balance-sheet
  lines (A06–A10) and the average rate for P&L lines (A11–A15) only when BOTH are printed AND the
  table is native; otherwise the one printed rate.
  FORMAT_3 → per row by its own stamp; a row whose unit or rate is not printed → null with
  "why_null": NO_PRINTED_RATE / UNIT_UNRESOLVED / RATE_ZERO_PLACEHOLDER / VALUE_ANNOTATED.
  Never take a rate from another filer, another year, or general knowledge.
4.4 FIELDS RESTATED per row: share_capital, reserves_surplus, total_assets, total_liabilities,
  investments, turnover (with "turnover_caption_used"), pbt, tax, pat, tnw = share_capital +
  reserves_surplus from the two restated values (null if either is null); Part B: investment amount,
  networth attributable, profit considered / not considered. Each carries a "formula" string, e.g.
  "2,135.1 (NGN Mn) × 0.0557547 ₹/NGN ÷ 10 = 11.90" or "35,450.10 (₹ cr) × 1 = 35,450.10". Two
  decimals; negatives stay negative; per-row FY carried over.
4.5 VERIFICATION, NOT PLACEMENT. After restating a row, test (a) SC + reserves + liabilities ≈
  assets under BOTH conventions (external liabilities; or gross liabilities = assets), (b) PBT − tax ≈
  PAT, (c) turnover vs total assets and vs the size prior (§2.10). A failed test is written into
  "checks" as a flag (IDENTITY_FAIL / SIGN_SUSPECT / MAGNITUDE_IMPLAUSIBLE / DOUBLE_CONVERSION_RISK /
  SCALE_SIGNATURE_×10|×100|×1000|×1e5|×1e7 / RATE_SIGNATURE) with the ratio observed. Flags never
  change a Part 1 value and never choose a Part 2 method — they only make the doubt visible.
4.6 CONFIDENCE per row: HIGH (FORMAT_1, or a printed INR twin), MEDIUM (native × a printed rate),
  LOW (FORMAT_3 resolved by a per-row stamp), NULL (no lock — the value is left null, never estimated).
